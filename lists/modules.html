rki_ml_pressure rki_ml_pressure.F90

Two BPG implementations: - ml_baroclinic_pressure_gradient (sigma method): differences internal pressure at the same sigma level. Cheap; PGE ~ dH/dx on steep bathymetry. - ml_baroclinic_pressure_gradient_jacobian (Shchepetkin & McWilliams 2003): interpolates neighbour-column pressure to the centre column’s z-mid level. Eliminates the leading sigma PGE; ~3x more expensive per cell. Both use the density anomaly rho - rho_ref(z) to keep the integrand small. rho_ref_at and all_neighbours_wet are private same-module helpers because the BPG kernels call them inside do concurrent, where NVHPC stdpar can’t lower cross-module calls.
rki_ml_pressure_unstr rki_ml_pressure_unstr.F90

Two BPG variants: * ml_baroclinic_pressure_gradient_unstr (sigma method): integrates the density anomaly bottom-up at the centre column, Green-Gauss horizontal gradient at constant sigma. Carries the sigma PGE. * ml_baroclinic_pressure_gradient_jacobian_unstr (Shchepetkin & McWilliams 2003): interpolates each neighbour column’s pressure to the physical depth of the centre column’s layer centre, then differences at constant z. Uniform density ⇒ zero gradient, eliminating the sigma PGE. Default for the regional path. Both subtract rho_ref(z) (from ml_compute_reference_density_unstr) before differencing to cut the residual sigma PGE in stratified water.
