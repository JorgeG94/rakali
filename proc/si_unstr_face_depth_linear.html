si_unstr_face_depth_linear

private pure function si_unstr_face_depth_linear(h_c, h_nbr) result(hf)

All-wet (2D barotropic) edge effective depth: the arithmetic mean of the two cells’ still-water depths, Hf = 0.5*(h_c + h_nbr). This is the gated-mean rule (face_depth_wetdry) in its all-wet limit — it reduces EXACTLY to the linear edge depth. Wall edges never call this (Hf stays 0); Dirichlet edges pass the ghost h.
