apply_manning_friction_unstr

Apply Manning bottom friction and surface wind stress to the centroid momenta (bundled to mirror the structured solver).

Friction (semi-implicit, unconditionally stable): c_f = gn^2/h^{1/3}; denom = 1 + dtc_f|u|/h; M /= denom. Wind stress (explicit): M += dttau/RHO_WATER, with the uniform state%wind_tau_{x,y} in Pa.

No-ops by default: friction skips when manning_n == 0, wind when both components are zero; both inactive returns immediately. Dry cells (depth below state%minimum_allowed_height) are skipped to avoid the 1/h^{4/3} singularity.
