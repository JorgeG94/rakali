Unstructured port of the structured A-grid KPP overlay (rki_ml_kpp). Same physics, adapted to the cell-centric (nz, ncells) layout and the two-pass (compute-then-apply) flux style the unstructured PP81 kernels use. Like the structured path, the closure is fused with the explicit-flux apply because the unstructured solver has no implicit vertical-diffusion solve:

  • ml_kpp_boundary_layer_unstr — per cell, computes u_* (from the barotropic wind stress), the surface buoyancy flux B_0, and the bulk-Richardson boundary-layer depth h_b (with the LMD94 eq-23 V_t^2 term). Stores all three on the multilayer state; kpp_bl_depth persists across steps to seed V_t^2.

  • ml_kpp_viscosity_unstr — momentum: nu_t = max(PP81 interior, KPP shape-function overlay), CFL-clamped explicit flux to hu/hv. Drop-in for ml_vertical_viscosity_unstr.

  • ml_kpp_diffusion_tracers_unstr — S + T: same overlay on the PP81 cubic diffusivity, plus the non-local counter-gradient transport folded into the interface flux. Drop-in for the two ml_vertical_diffusion_tracer_unstr calls.

When the overlay is inactive (no wind + no destabilizing flux ⇒ w_s = 0), every interface falls through to the bit-identical PP81 arithmetic, so vmix_use_kpp off leaves existing results unchanged. u_* comes from the spatially-uniform state%barotropic%wind_tau_x/y (the unstructured wind-stress coupling); a per-cell wind field is a future enhancement.

Layer convention is bottom-up (k=1 bed, k=nz surface), matching the rest of the unstructured multilayer path.

public pure subroutine ml_kpp_boundary_layer_unstr(state, hS_layer)

Pass 1: per-cell u_, B_0, and the bulk-Ri boundary-layer depth. Mirrors ml_kpp_boundary_layer (structured) on the cell-centric layout. u_ uses the uniform barotropic wind stress; B_0 uses the per-cell surface heat / virtual-salt fluxes.
