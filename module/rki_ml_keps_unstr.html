Unstructured (triangular) port of the structured rki_ml_keps: the same clean-room k-epsilon closure (Launder-Spalding; Rodi 1987; Warner 2005) per water column, cell-centric on the unstructured C-grid.

dk/dt = d/dz(nu_t/sig_k dk/dz) + P + B - eps deps/dt = d/dz(nu_t/sig_e deps/dz) + (eps/k)(ce1 P + ce3 B - ce2 eps) nu_t = c_mu k^2/eps, kappa_t = c_mu’ k^2/eps

Staggering (bottom-up, ROMS): velocity/density at layer centres (1..nz); k/eps/nu_t/kappa_t at interfaces (1..nz+1, 1=bed, nz+1=surface). Per cell: implicit (Thomas) vertical diffusion + semi-implicit (Patankar) dissipation sinks for positivity. Stability functions come from the shared rki_keps_stability (constant / Galperin / Canuto) as a pure !$acc routine seq (cross-module — a cheap device call, not inlined).

The kernel is a flat explicit-shape _impl (arrays as (nz, ncell)) invoked through a thin state-passed shim — keeps the device loop free of derived-type dereferences and is directly unit-testable like the structured ml_keps_update. public for unit testing public for unit testing public for unit testing

public pure subroutine ml_keps_friction_velocities_unstr(state)

Per-cell surface + bed friction velocities feeding the keps wall functions. Surface: u_s = sqrt(|tau_wind|/rho_0) from the (uniform) unstructured wind stress. Bed: quadratic Manning u_b = sqrt(C_d)*|u_b|, C_d = g n^2 / H^(1/3), from the bottom (k=1) layer speed. Writes keps_ustar_s/b on the multilayer state.

public subroutine ml_keps_update_unstr(state, dt, use_walls)

State-passed shim: advance k/eps one step per cell and recover nu_t/kappa_t. use_walls imposes the Dirichlet log-law wall functions (from keps_ustar_s/b); .false. uses the zero-gradient closure (the standalone unit tests). Extracts the flat arrays and calls the impl.

private pure subroutine keps_tridiag_solve_noflux_u(a, b, c, rhs, cp, nn)
