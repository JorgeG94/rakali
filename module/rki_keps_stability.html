keps_stability_funcs

Stratification-aware k-epsilon stability functions (S^2/N^2 -> c_mu/c_mu’), shared by structured (rki_ml_keps) and unstructured (rki_ml_keps_unstr). CONSTANT: c_mu = 0.09. GALPERIN: Galperin et al. (1988) / Mellor-Yamada level-2.5 algebraic form. CANUTO: Canuto et al. (2001) Model A in the rational alpha_M/alpha_N form of Umlauf & Burchard (2003).

public pure subroutine keps_stability_funcs(scheme, s2, n2, k, e, cmu_o, cmup_o)

Stability functions (c_mu, c_mu’) for the chosen scheme, given shear S^2 and buoyancy N^2 at the interface. Result dummies are cmu_o/cmup_o, NOT cmu/cmup: a cmu dummy shadows the module parameter CMU (case-insensitive) -> self-assignment of undefined intent(out) -> nu_t floored on GPU/gfortran.
