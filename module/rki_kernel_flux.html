Net flux divergence for the shallow water equations via HLL or HLLC Riemann solver with hydrostatic reconstruction (Audusse et al. 2004) and minmod slope-limited reconstruction. HLL/HLLC differ only in the transverse momentum flux. Free surface eta = h + B is reconstructed (not h) for the well-balanced property.
