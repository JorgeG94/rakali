Generic tracer pipeline (advection, distribute, RK2 save/average, clamp, BCs) plus tracer-side mixing (vertical exchange, vertical diffusion, horizontal diffusion). All entry points take a tracer field as an actual argument (hTr_layer) and update it in place, so the same kernel serves salinity, temperature, and any future passive tracer.

public pure subroutine ml_init_tracer_stratified_unstr(state, hTr_layer, tr_bottom, tr_surface)

Seed a tracer with a linear vertical profile: tr_bottom at k=1 (bed) to tr_surface at k=nz (surface), per the bottom-up layer convention. The per-column entry point anticipated by ml_init_tracer_unstr. The profile is computed inline from the two scalars (no host-side per-layer array) so the seed runs correctly on the device after enter_data. Caller guarantees nz > 1. hTr_k(cell) = h_layer_k(cell) * [tr_bottom + dtr*(k - 1)]

public pure subroutine ml_seed_standard_tracers_unstr(state)

Seed the two standard tracers (salinity, temperature) from the IC scalars carried on state%multilayer, each dispatching uniform-vs- stratified via ml_seed_tracer_unstr. Call once after ml_init_layers_unstr and before EOS. The unstructured twin of the structured multilayer_state_t%seed_tracers; a free helper (not a type-bound method) because the unstructured tracer kernels take the top-level unstr_state_t, so binding on the state would close a state <-> tracers_unstr module cycle.

public pure subroutine ml_seed_tracer_unstr(state, hTr_layer, tr_uniform, tr_bottom, tr_surface)

Seed one tracer’s hTr field, dispatching between the uniform and the linear-stratified profile from the tracer’s IC scalars. When both tr_bottom and tr_surface are set (non-zero) and nz > 1 a linear profile is laid down from tr_bottom at the bed (k=1) to tr_surface at the surface (k=nz); otherwise every layer is filled with tr_uniform. The single stratified-vs-uniform gate shared by the unstructured IC seed site (solver_unstr_init_multilayer). Mirrors the structured ml_seed_tracer.
