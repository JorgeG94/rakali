Container for the spatially-varying surface forcing fields applied by the barotropic and multilayer pipelines: wind stress, atmospheric pressure, precipitation, net heat flux, and the virtual freshwater flux (E - P) used as a virtual salt flux on the top layer.

Distinct from forcing_t in rki_forcing (the NetCDF reader that fills these); this is the on-state container kernels read each step.
