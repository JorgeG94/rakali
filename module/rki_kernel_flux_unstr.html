Net flux divergence at every owned cell via the Kurganov-Noelle-Petrova central-upwind scheme on triangles.

Cell-centric: each triangle loops its three edges, computes the central-upwind flux from both sides, and accumulates ONLY into its own update arrays. Each interior edge is visited twice (once per neighbour) – no scatter, no race, no atomics – which is what makes the kernel safe for do concurrent on GPU. Boundary edges (mesh%neighbours(i, k) < 0) dispatch per-edge BC state; group 0 falls through to BC_WALL (normal-momentum mirror). The well-balanced gravity bed-slope source is added per cell, then accumulators are normalised by area to flux divergence (per unit area), ready to be multiplied by dt in the update step.

private pure subroutine compute_chapman_eta_target(state, mesh, k, i, hle, eta_target)

Orlanski-style radiating ghost stage at boundary edge i of cell k. Advects the persistent chapman_eta_old(i, k) toward the interior centroid stage along the phase speed, writes back the new eta_old, and returns eta_target as the BC_TIDAL ghost eta. Per-edge writes to chapman_eta_old(i, k) are race-free: each (i, k) is touched only by cell k.
