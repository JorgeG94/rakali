High-frequency time-series output at fixed gauge locations.

Each gauge is located in a triangle via point-in-triangle search at init time. Barycentric coordinates are precomputed and cached for fast interpolation at each write. Output is CF-1.8 station NetCDF with dimensions (station, time).

Gauges outside every cell are silently skipped.
