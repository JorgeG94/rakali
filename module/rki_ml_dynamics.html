Barotropic-baroclinic coupling and vertical structure: distribute barotropic acceleration to layers (preserving shear), maintain layer thickness (sigma constraint or vcoord remap), diagnostic w + interface momentum exchange, layer-state RK2 plumbing and BC distribution. Owns the persistent vcoord-remap workspace; teardown via ml_dynamics_workspaces_cleanup.

public subroutine ml_apply_layer_bcs_full(h_layer, hu_layer, hv_layer, h, hu, hv, dsig, vcoord_z_ref_col, h_min, grid, nz)

VCOORD_ZSTAR_FULL variant of ml_apply_layer_bcs. Rebuilds ghost-cell h_layer from the per-column vcoord_z_ref_col profile (as the interior ml_distribute_with_remap_full) so the layout is consistent across the boundary — a sigma fallback would mismatch and make CWC advection inject tracer at the wrong per-layer ratio. Momentum is still split by dsig(k) (ghost barotropic momentum carries no internal shear).

public pure subroutine ml_apply_wind_to_surface_layer(hu_layer, hv_layer, h_layer, wind_x, wind_y, grid, nz, dt)

Apply the surface wind stress to the TOP layer (k=nz) momentum only, so the wind is surface-concentrated and generates vertical shear that the vertical mixing then spreads downward — instead of the depth-uniform barotropic forcing (apply_friction). tau = (wind_x, wind_y) in Pa; adds dt*tau/RHO_WATER to hu/hv_layer(:,:,nz). Gated by wind_surface_layer; the caller suppresses the barotropic wind so the stress is not applied twice.

public subroutine ml_distribute_with_remap(h, hu, hv, hu_pre, hv_pre, coord_type, dsig, z_ref, depth_transition, blend_width, h_layer, hu_layer, hv_layer, grid, nz)

Barotropic-to-layer distribution with vertical-coordinate reshaping — momentum + thickness only. Saves old thicknesses into remap_dz_old, sets new target h_layer via vcoord, distributes the barotropic acceleration. Per-tracer remap is done separately by ml_remap_tracer (looped over the registry by the solver). All array args must be device-resident under GPU offload; vcoord_t is NOT passed (raw arrays/scalars instead) to avoid allocatable-component resolution issues on the device.

public subroutine ml_remap_tracer(hTr, h, remap_method, h_layer, grid, nz)

Conservatively remap ONE tracer from the saved pre-update layer thicknesses (remap_dz_old) onto the current h_layer (new vcoord target): scale dz_old to sum to H_new, column-mean fallback at vanishing layers, conservative remap_column. Looped over the tracer registry by the solver. Must run AFTER the dynamics remap (which writes remap_dz_old and the new h_layer).
