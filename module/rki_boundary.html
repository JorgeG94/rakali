private pure subroutine bc_chapman_edge(h, hu, hv, b, grid, ed, eta_old, dt, dx, dy)

Chapman / Orlanski radiation for one edge. The ghost surface advects at a clamped, non-negative phase speed estimated from the interior eta tendency and along-normal gradient; the momentum is the Flather radiation about that surface (sign ed%nrm_sign). eta_old is this edge’s persistent surface (length n_tan), refreshed to the interior eta on the k==1 layer (per-t writes are disjoint). dd = dx on W/E, dy on S/N.

private pure subroutine bc_flather_edge(h, hu, hv, b, grid, ed, eta_ext, prescribe_h)

Flather radiation for one edge. Shared by OPEN (eta_ext = 0, prescribe_h = .false. -> zero-gradient ghost depth) and TIDAL (eta_ext = tidal_eta, prescribe_h = .true. -> ghost depth from the prescribed surface). The NORMAL momentum radiates the surface anomaly outward (sign from ed%nrm_sign); the tangential momentum is copied from the first interior cell.
